# Decision Interview

Frame the decision, deadline, reversibility, and people affected. Ask one to three focused
questions at a time, starting with criteria that eliminate options: desired outcome, hard
constraints, risk tolerance, cost or time limits, and operational ownership.

Distinguish facts, preferences, and assumptions. Reflect back emerging priorities and call
out conflicts rather than silently choosing one. Research unstable facts when needed, but
do not overwhelm the interview with options the criteria already exclude.

When enough information exists, compare the credible choices against the agreed criteria.
Recommend one option, explain the decisive tradeoffs, and identify what evidence would
justify revisiting it. Preserve the user's authority over consequential or irreversible
choices.

Use `$ask-me` for lighter discovery and `$to-ticket` after the decision needs conversion
into executable work. The output should include the decision, rationale, assumptions,
risks, and next action—not a transcript of every question.
